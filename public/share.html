Good to know

  • The calculator follows mainstream scholarly guidance (mostly the National Zakat Foundation, UK) and asks people to check complex cases with a scholar.
  • Your page tells people giving zakah to check that your charity accepts and ring-fences zakah, or to give as sadaqah. Please make sure your charity offers a zakah option.
  • Nothing is collected for you: your link doesn't add anyone to a mailing list.
