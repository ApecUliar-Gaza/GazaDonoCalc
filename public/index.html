About this calculator

  • Rate: 2.5% of your net zakatable wealth, once it has stayed above the nisab for a lunar year.
  • Nisab: the value of 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver, using today's prices. Silver gives a lower threshold, which is safer and better for the poor.
  • Not zakatable: your home, car, furniture, clothes and other things for personal use; defined-benefit and state pensions.
  • Where scholars differ (jewellery, debts, pensions, long-term shares, crypto), we show the mainstream view and tell you what we did. You can change these choices in the settings.
  • Who can receive zakah: only the eight groups named in the Qur'an (9:60), such as the poor and those in need. Always give through a charity that keeps zakah separate.

This tool is a guide based on mainstream scholarly views, mostly following the National Zakat Foundation (UK). It is not a fatwa. If your situation is complex, or you follow a particular madhab, please confirm with a qualified scholar.
