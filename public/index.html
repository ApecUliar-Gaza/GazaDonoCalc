Debts you can deduct

Only debts due within the next 12 months, or the next 12 months of payments on a mortgage or long-term loan (not the interest).

Has your wealth been above the nisab for a full lunar year?

Zakah is due once a year, on the anniversary of the day your wealth first reached the nisab. If you don't know the date, pick your best estimate (many people use Ramadan). Money you received during the year is added in on that date and doesn't need a year of its own (the Hanafi view, followed by NZF), so answer about your wealth as a whole.

About this calculator

2.5%of your net zakatable wealth, once it has stayed above the nisab for a lunar year.
Nisabthe value of 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver at today's prices. Silver gives a lower threshold, which is safer and better for the poor.
Not countedyour home, car, furniture, clothes and personal items; defined-benefit and state pensions.
Who receives itonly the eight groups named in the Qur'an (9:60), such as the poor and those in need. Give through a charity that keeps zakah separate.

Where scholars differ (jewellery, debts, pensions, long-term shares, crypto), we show the mainstream view, say what we did, and let you change it in the settings.

This tool is a guide based on mainstream scholarly views, mostly following the National Zakat Foundation (UK). It is not a fatwa. If your situation is complex, or you follow a particular madhab, please confirm with a qualified scholar.
